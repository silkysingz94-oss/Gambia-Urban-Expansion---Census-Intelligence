METHODOLOGY AND INTERPRETATION
Built-up area: GHSL built_surface values are summed within EA boundaries. Values are converted from square metres to hectares.

Built-up share: total built-up surface divided by EA land area, multiplied by 100.

Built-up change: selected-year built-up area minus comparison-year built-up area, reported in hectares and as a percentage of the comparison year.

Built-up m² per person: built-up surface divided by 2023 census population.

Built-up m² per household: built-up surface divided by 2023 census households.

Population density on built-up land: census population divided by built-up area in hectares.

Important: these are built-up surface efficiency proxies, not a complete measure of urban land-use efficiency. They do not represent the full land parcel occupied by buildings.

GHSL 2025 and 2030 are modelled estimates/projections. They must not be presented as direct observations.
